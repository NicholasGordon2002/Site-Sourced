Privacy — Delivered Fixture Barber Shop

How Delivered Fixture Barber Shop handles a message sent from this website.

Who we are

Delivered Fixture Barber Shop, Hamilton, ON. Privacy contact: hello@delivered-fixture-barber.ca.

What this page is

This is Delivered Fixture Barber Shop's own website. The contact form is set up in Delivered Fixture Barber Shop's own account, so the message comes straight to Delivered Fixture Barber Shop. Site Sourced does not receive a copy of anything you send and does not run this website.

What is collected

The form records what you type into it: your name, your phone number, your preferred service, your preferred day or days, your preferred time, your email address if you give one and your message if you add one.

The form service also records what comes with any web form, without you typing it: your IP address (the number that identifies the connection you are using), an approximate location worked out from that address, and basic request information such as your browser and the page or site you came from.

There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking on this page.

Why it is collected

To read your message and reply to you once. Your details are not used for anything else and are not added to a mailing list.

Sending the form is not consent to any marketing.

Where it goes

The form is handled by Formspark, a third-party form service. Formspark emails it to Delivered Fixture Barber Shop and also keeps a copy in Delivered Fixture Barber Shop's own Formspark account until Delivered Fixture Barber Shop deletes it.

Formspark operates internationally, so the message may be handled under the laws of the places where its servers sit.

A time booked from this site is handled by the booking service Delivered Fixture Barber Shop uses, on that service's own page and under its own privacy policy. Site Sourced is not involved in a booking and receives no part of it.

How long it is kept

Your message is kept in Formspark's account until it is deleted there, and Formspark never removes it by itself. A message its spam filter sets aside is kept for 12 months and cannot be deleted earlier, and a deleted message stays recoverable in Formspark's records for a further 30 days. Formspark also keeps the IP address and approximate location it recorded for 12 months, whether or not your message is still there.

The account this form sends to is Delivered Fixture Barber Shop's own, and Delivered Fixture Barber Shop deletes messages from it. We do not run that account and cannot promise a timetable for it. Email hello@delivered-fixture-barber.ca to ask for yours to be deleted.

Your choices

Email hello@delivered-fixture-barber.ca to ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. If Formspark's spam filter is holding a message, only Formspark can remove it, and Formspark releases it after 12 months.

Complaints

Email hello@delivered-fixture-barber.ca first. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
